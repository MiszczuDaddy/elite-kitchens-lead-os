# Receptionist: the live review of 1 Nov 2026

Thomas ran `scripts/receptionist-week.js` and the five real conversations of that week (14 automatic messages) were read. No factual error was found: no price quoted, no warranty, no promised availability, it stopped when Thomas took over and ignored thank-yous. The faults were in how it talked. Four repairs:

1. **No asking twice.** The owner's greeting question in the Knowledge Centre (`still_looking`, "Are you looking to arrange a consultation for your new kitchen?") already offers a consultation, but a "yes" to it still got "Would you like us to call out and have a look at your space?". Now, when that line offers a visit (it mentions a consultation, visit, call-out…) and the customer's answer is a yes ("yep", "probably yes", "yes please", "if you can", "that would be great") with no no/not in it, it counts as agreement and the days are asked next. With the default line ("Are you still looking at a new kitchen?") nothing changed: a yes still gets the offer, once. The staff draft flow is untouched.
2. **A shop's own away message is not a person.** A business WhatsApp account can answer our welcome by itself ("Thank you for contacting …! Please let us know how we can help you…"). If the customer's only messages so far are such a message (`functions/lib/autoReply.js`: an explicit away/automatic message, or "thank you for contacting/messaging" plus a second sign such as an offer of help, a promise to reply, a link or a long text), it arrived within 3 minutes of our welcome and nothing else has been written, the receptionist does nothing: no AI call, nothing sent, nobody alerted, the chat is not paused, and the message is marked handled. The lead's real answer is answered as usual. A customer's own "thanks for getting in touch" or "I'm currently away" never matches.
3. **No "Perfect".** The acknowledgement is now "Thanks, I'll pass that on to the team and we'll confirm a time with you shortly." ("Perfect" sounded as if the day they suggested, or a question they asked, was settled.)
4. **After 20:00 Irish time** the automatic acknowledgement says "…we'll confirm a time with you tomorrow." instead of "shortly". A staff draft (a person reads it) always has the daytime wording.

Not changed: the Knowledge Centre lines, the quiet hours, the daily cap, the answer checker, or anything the receptionist may say about prices.
